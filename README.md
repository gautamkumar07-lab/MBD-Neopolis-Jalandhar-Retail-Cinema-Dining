# MBD Neopolis Jalandhar

**A Shopping, Dining & Entertainment Destination**

## About the Business

MBD Neopolis Jalandhar is a shopping and entertainment destination located on Main G.T. Road, adjacent to the Radisson Hotel near BMC Chowk, Jalandhar, Punjab, India.

The mall brings together retail stores, fashion and lifestyle brands, a PVR multiplex, food and beverage outlets, wellness services, a gaming zone, and multilevel parking under one roof.

## Key Features

- Four-level retail development
- Four-screen PVR multiplex with Gold Class seating
- Shopping and lifestyle brands
- Gigabite food court
- Indian and international dining options
- Wellness and spa services
- Gaming and entertainment zone
- Multilevel parking
